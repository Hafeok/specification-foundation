# canon/

The canonical artefacts of the specification foundation: the construct list, the resolution
condition, the boundary declarations, and the conformance criterion when it lands.

**Empty as at 2026-09-01.** Nothing is filed yet. Placement is proposed at Gate 3 of the seeding
session and executed at Gate 4; nothing lands here that was not ratified there. The conformance
criterion is a later session's entirely.

**What files here — ruled at `CG-R-11`.** A file lands under `canon/` only if it is **itself a
determination**: the construct list, the resolution condition, the boundary declarations, the
conformance criterion. The test is what the artefact *is for*, not what it is about. Provenance
about canon — however load-bearing — is evidential and files under `evidence/`; the supersession
record files there.

What will be true of anything filed here:

- **Filed, not authored.** The seeding session files ratified-for-filing artefacts as they
  arrived; it writes none of them, and it resolves no inconsistency it finds in them.
- **Status on its face.** Every artefact carries its own register marker; everything arriving from
  the seed bundle is `[PROPOSED]` by its own header, and filing does not promote it.
- **Supersession never rewrites** (`CG-rule-02`, cited not restated). The construct list has moved
  twice since it was written; both the original state and the superseding record will be visible
  here, because the falsifier's category list derives from the original and the derivation needs
  both states.
