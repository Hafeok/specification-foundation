# Term registry

The vocabulary of record for this repository's own structural terms. An entry states what a term
denotes here, and — where a ruling fixed the meaning — cites it. Terms are added by ratification,
never by usage drifting into place.

**Scope note.** This registry belongs to the specification projection. The software projection
(`decision-driven-design`) keeps its own; that is an accepted cost of the peer relation (S-1), and
drift between the two registries is a real risk. **No drift check exists, and none is committed
here** — ruled at `CG-R-12`: drift between two peer registries is a two-party problem neither
peer owns, and a cross-repository check belongs in `canon-governance`, which currently issues no
verdicts at all. Recorded as an **unowned gap**, dated 2026-09-01.

---

## layer

**Projection depth only**: actor-general → software → sector. Determinations added, never
contradicted, forward only.

Per ruling **R-2**. *Layer* says nothing about granularity: it is one of two **independent
coordinates, not a hierarchy** — see the joint statement below.

## scale

**Granularity of act composition within a projection**: fine to coarse. Acts compose across
scales.

Per ruling **R-2**. *Scale* says nothing about projection depth: it is one of two **independent
coordinates, not a hierarchy** — see the joint statement below.

---

## Layer and scale are independent coordinates

Stated in the registry because the natural reading of two ordered axes is that they nest, and they
do not. **Nothing about a position on one implies anything about the other.** A sector-specific
act at fine scale and an actor-general act at coarse scale are both well formed. Anything
reasoning from a layer to a scale, or the reverse, is reasoning from a hierarchy that does not
exist. (R-2; the resolution condition is evaluated at one scale, within one projection — the two
constraints are separate because the axes are.)
