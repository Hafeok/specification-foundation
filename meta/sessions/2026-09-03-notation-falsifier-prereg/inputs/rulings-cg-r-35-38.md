## CG-R-35 — The list is confirmed by blind re-derivation, not by inspection

The weakest point is correctly named and it is the one that matters: the list and the set have the same author, a gate apart, and nothing fixes the list before the set the way CG-rule-08 fixes it before results. The derivation grid and the rejection register are a real independence proxy, and they are a proxy.

The test proposed — *does this row survive as a decision point for a command slice about anything, with the eleven determinations forgotten?* — is the right test. It cannot be applied by the author, who cannot forget the set, and it should not be applied by the ruling authority on a summary of the rows.

**Ruled: a blind re-derivation.** A separate session is given the derivation grid, the act type, and the four admission conditions, and **not** the determination set, not the Gate 1 output, and not the proposed list. It produces its own category list by the same method and stops.

The two lists are then compared:

- **Rows in both** — the derivation is reproducible and the row is not set-fitted.
- **Rows only in the proposed list** — candidates for set-fitting. Each is defended on the derivation alone or struck.
- **Rows only in the re-derivation** — candidates for omission. Each is admitted or refused on the admission conditions.

Divergence is the finding, in both directions. A large overlap is evidence the method carries the list rather than the author; a small one means the grid does not determine what it appeared to determine, which is a finding about the method and is worth more than a ratified list.

The re-derivation is filed with the proposed list, and the reconciliation is ratified before Gate 3.

**Cost, stated:** one session and a delay to Gate 3. The category list is the denominator of the entire measure and the artefact most likely to be fitted. This is the cheapest available independence and it is worth the gate.

## CG-R-36 — The layer pre-registration re-derives. Only the method transfers.

The finding is correct and it corrects me. `prereg-notation-inference-load.md` §10 claims the category list is the byproduct the layer pre-registration defers to a separate session. That is wrong: the layer claim's corpus is SPETLR, whose act types are Extractor, Transformer and Loader, and rows drawn from a command slice's five stages do not transfer to a Transformer.

**Ruled:** §10 is amended. What transfers is **the derivation method** — the stage-by-construct grid, the four admission conditions, the rejection register, and the blind re-derivation of CG-R-35. The layer pre-registration re-derives its own list for its own act types, by this method.

Its corpus does not change. SPETLR was chosen because it encodes almost nothing, which remains the right reason.

This weakens the ordering argument that put the notation claim first, and does not overturn it: the layer claim inherits an exercised method rather than an exercised list, which is less than claimed and still real.

## CG-R-37 — The frame is instances, and its size is set-dependent

Accepted, with a consequence Gate 3 must carry.

One decision point per category, instantiated at closure into the points the set exhibits, is right — a category is a kind of decision and the frame must be the decisions themselves.

But the instance count then depends on the determination set. Both arms share the set, so there is no bias between them. What it means is that **frame size is not a property of the act type alone**, and the smallest detectable effect Gate 3 must state is computed against the instantiated frame, not against nineteen.

Roughly twenty-three instances is a materially better position than the small frame CG-R-33 anticipated, and Gate 3 should say what that does to detectability rather than letting the earlier pessimism stand unrevised.

## CG-R-38 — The leakage check is accepted; paraphrase leakage is permanent

Verbatim whole-word checking across both arms, the structured-prose arm and the base statement, with category names avoiding every schema key and enum value: accepted and written out.

Paraphrase leakage is unreachable by that check and is **stated as a permanent limit**, not carried as a gap awaiting an instrument. A category cued by a synonym is cued, and no verbatim check finds it. This is the same class as meaning across the seam and as whether a discrimination pair differs for its stated reason: checked by reading, by a named principal, or not at all.
