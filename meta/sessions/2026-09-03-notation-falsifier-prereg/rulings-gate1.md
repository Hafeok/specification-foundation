> ## CG-R-29 — Closure adds residual declarations only
>
> Accepted as recommended. A determination added once the frame is visible tunes the determined partition to the denominator, which is the same defect as adjusting the category list after results.
>
> **Ruled:** closure may add **declared residuals** and nothing else. If a frame category is neither settled by the eleven nor declarable as residual without adding a determination, that is reported as a Gate 2 finding, not resolved by writing one.
>
> ---
>
> ## CG-R-30 — Base delivery is an identical prose statement to both arms
>
> The event model is YAML. Giving arm P's actor YAML beside prose contaminates the control with the notation under test; the base must be held constant and must not be in the treatment's form.
>
> **Ruled:** both arms receive the slice as **one short prose statement, byte-identical across arms**, carrying only what the slice is — name, what it reads, what it writes. Not the determinations, which are the treatment.
>
> Two constraints on it: it must not contain frame category names, per the leakage guard; and it is fixed and hashed at Gate 3 with the other pre-registered items.
>
> ---
>
> ## CG-R-31 — Explicit override gets its own cell. Authority is named as a separate hypothesis.
>
> The weakest point is correctly identified and is the real cost of CG-R-22. A mismatch against a counter-conventional determination is ambiguous between *did not retrieve* and *retrieved and overrode*.
>
> **Ruled, in three parts.**
>
> **Explicit override is a fourth cell at Gate 4** — the output resolves against the determination and says that it is doing so. Visible, and it must not be scored as inference.
>
> **Explicit override rate is reported per arm**, and is the observable proxy for the authority effect. If it differs materially between arms while the primary measure also differs, authority is a live alternative explanation and the result is reported with that stated. If override rates are comparable and the primary measure differs, authority is a weaker explanation — not excluded, weakened.
>
> **Silent override is unmeasurable and stays that way.** It scores inferred, by construction, and no choice of determinations removes it. Stated as a permanent limit wherever the result is cited.
>
> **And name it properly:** an actor treating a schema-typed record as more binding than prose may be a real benefit of the notation. It is a **different claim** from inference load, this design cannot test it, and it must not be absorbed into a pass. Record it as a candidate hypothesis rather than a nuisance.
>
> ---
>
> ## CG-R-32 — The counter-conventional subset minimum is fixed on the departs basis
>
> Accepted. A matched departure is stronger evidence of retrieval than a matched count, because a conventional determination can be matched by defaulting.
>
> **Ruled:** Gate 3 fixes the minimum on the *departs* basis alone. The two determinations carrying non-defaultable specifics are recorded distinctly — matching a specific no default supplies is the strongest single retrieval signal in the set, and it should be visible in the analysis rather than pooled.
>
> ---
>
> ## CG-R-33 — Coarse detectability is accepted; runs increase, scope does not
>
> The act has one ground and one verdict, both internal, so the frame will be small and each classification moves the rate materially.
>
> **Ruled:** accepted. Gate 3 raises **runs per arm**, not acts. Adding a second act would improve granularity and would break the single-act design, introduce a second frame, and turn a session into a project.
>
> **Stated at Gate 3 and wherever the result is cited:** the smallest effect this design can detect, and whether it is smaller than the effect worth caring about. If it is not, that is a finding about the design and should be reported before execution rather than after.
>
> ---
>
> ## CG-R-34 — Boundary declarations are out of scope, and this is a substantive limit
>
> The set exercises no external ground and no terminal verdict, so the result says nothing about whether the notation helps with boundary declarations.
>
> That is not a minor gap. Boundary declaration is a large part of what the notation adds over the base — it is DP-7, it is what makes the resolution condition sound, and it is where the composition check lives.
>
> **Ruled:** accepted as a scope limit for this experiment, stated explicitly in the pre-registration and in any citation of the result. Not repaired by extending the set: an act that does not cross a boundary should not have one bolted on to widen the frame.
>
